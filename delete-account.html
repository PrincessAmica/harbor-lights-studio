Delete your account

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion of your Anchor account and its cloud data by emailing Harbor Lights Studio. You do not need to install or open Anchor to submit a request.

Request account deletion

You can also send an email yourself to harborlightsstudio@proton.me with the subject Delete my Anchor account and include the email address associated with your Anchor account.

Preferably send the request from that address. If you cannot access it, explain that in your message so we can discuss how to verify ownership. Never send your password or backup passphrase.

What happens next

  1. Harbor Lights Studio Support receives and reviews your request.
  2. We verify account ownership where necessary to prevent someone else deleting your account.
  3. We delete the account and associated active cloud records, then send confirmation.

We aim to handle emailed deletion requests within 7 days. If we need more information or there is a delay, we will contact you. Sending an email starts a request; it does not instantly delete the account.

What account deletion removes

  • Your Anchor authentication account and its associated account information.
  • Your account’s active cloud plan and journal backups, including encrypted backup content.
  • Your account’s stored backup-key records and related backup metadata.

Deletion is permanent. Save any content you want to keep before requesting it. Provider logs and recovery copies have separate retention schedules and may not disappear immediately. Routine request correspondence is manually deleted 30 days after final resolution, unless a longer period is necessary for an unresolved legal or security matter. See Impressum & Privacy — retention and deletion.

Offline copies and exported files

An emailed cloud-account deletion request does not remotely erase plans or journal entries stored on your phones, or files you exported or shared.

In Anchor’s in-app account-deletion flow, you can choose whether to retain this account’s plans and journal entries as guest copies on that phone. Existing guest data is kept.

If you also want to remove offline data, use the appropriate app controls or clear Anchor’s app storage in Android settings. Delete exported files separately from wherever you saved them.

Delete from inside Anchor

  1. Open the account screen using the profile button.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete and select Delete account.
  3. Review whether you want to keep offline copies on that phone.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm permanent deletion. Keep Anchor open while it processes the request.

If deletion reports an error or remains pending, retry as directed in the app or contact us at the email address above.
